# AI_CONTEXT.md

This document captures the high‑level context for the **Token of Halawa** project, including its purpose, target audience, and key design goals.

- **Purpose**: Provide a modern, lightweight donation platform with a developer panel for managing campaigns, banners, and donor data.
- **Target Users**: NGOs, student groups, and community organizers.
- **Key Goals**: Simplicity, performance, privacy, and a clean UI without heavy CSS frameworks.
